Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about river water surveillance
What is Smart River Water Level and Quality Surveillance?
It is a real-time GIS-based web platform that monitors water quality and flood levels across 40+ Indian river monitoring stations using live CPCB RTWQMS telemetry data. The dashboard provides instant Water Quality Index (WQI), flood alerts, and 12-parameter water analysis.
Which rivers are monitored in this system?
Major Indian rivers monitored include Ganga, Yamuna, Brahmaputra, Godavari, Krishna, Narmada, Cauvery, Mahanadi, Sabarmati, Punpun, Damodar, Baitarani and many more across multiple states of India.
What is Water Quality Index (WQI) and how is it calculated?
WQI is a single score from 0 to 100 representing overall river health. It is calculated using the Weighted Arithmetic Water Quality Index (WAWQI) method across 12 parameters: Dissolved Oxygen (DO), BOD, COD, pH, Turbidity, TDS, Nitrate, Conductivity, Temperature, Ammonia, Fecal Coliform, and Total Coliform — as per BIS IS 10500 standards.
What data source does this system use?
The system fetches live telemetry data directly from CPCB RTWQMS (Central Pollution Control Board – Real Time Water Quality Monitoring Stations) server at rtwqmsdb1.cpcb.gov.in. A resilient fallback dataset ensures 100% uptime even when government servers are temporarily unavailable.
How does the flood alert system work?
The system continuously monitors water level readings against pre-defined thresholds for each station. When a parameter like DO drops below 4 mg/L or turbidity exceeds safe limits, an instant color-coded alert is triggered on the dashboard map with red/orange pins and warning banners.
Can I download or print inspection reports?
Yes! You can generate a 1-click A4 PDF inspection report for any monitoring station directly from the dashboard. The report includes all 12 water quality parameters, WQI score, station metadata, and timestamp — ready for official use by CPCB/SPCB officers.
Is this platform free to use?
Yes, this platform is completely free for public use. It is an open-source initiative developed by Team FUN LINES for Smart India Hackathon 2026, aimed at promoting environmental awareness and transparent river health monitoring across India.
